The First Colour

A burnt-out painter steps through a shop door into a city stuck in twilight. To get home she has to find her inspiration again, and the way she treats Kessa, a cat-girl everyone calls a thief, decides which of five endings she reaches.

  • Wrote the whole branching story in Ink: about 300 lines, a trust system and five endings
  • Built all gameplay logic with Unity Visual Scripting: quest steps, dialogue with portraits and backgrounds, story saved between scenes
  • Designed eight scenes with fixed cameras and Timeline cutscenes
  • Added a main menu, 14 achievements and film-style credits

First-person puzzle · Solo · ARU

Kirk's Notes

A maze full of locked rooms and colour-key doors. Notes left on the walls help you solve each puzzle. Later you find a gun to deal with turrets, scavenge health and ammo, and near the end you find Kirk, the man who wrote the notes. Your time sets your final score.

  • Designed the maze layout and the puzzle chain
  • Wrote the notes that guide the player and tell Kirk's story
  • Built colour keys and doors, turrets, pickups, minimap and timer-based scoring

3D puzzle · Solo · ARU

Robot Teleport Puzzle

The brief: take an existing robot puzzle template and add a new mechanic that works with the old ones. I added teleporters that switch on with buttons and pressure plates, then hid a secret ending: a button at the end of the level opens a teleport back near the start, where a hidden reward waits.

  • Designed the teleport mechanic and how it combines with robot printing and orbs
  • Implemented buttons, pressure plates and teleporters in Blueprints
  • Planned the level route and the secret ending

A four-level platformer with health, a timer and a score. Collect apples and dodge spikes, spinning saws and moving crushers. On the final level the knight learns to throw fireballs that break walls and destroy enemies.

About

Full CV →

I'm Mykola, a second-year Computer Games Design student at Anglia Ruskin University in Cambridge. I'm originally from the Ivano-Frankivsk region of Ukraine.

Before games, I worked in busy London hotels and banquet halls. That's where I learned to work fast in a team and stay calm when everything happens at once, which turns out to be useful on game projects too.

I'm most interested in narrative and level design: a choice, a note on a wall or a hidden button that changes how you see the whole game.
